1000 Robux terms

If we build your idea, you get 1000 Robux. That only happens if all of this is true:

  1. The game is finished and published publicly on Roblox. Payout is after release, not while it’s still in development.
  2. The game has been live for at least one week from the launch date.
  3. When we email you, you send back the Roblox username you want paid to.

If your idea is selected, we email the address you put on the form. You reply with a username, and we send the Robux straight to that profile.

If more than one person sends the same idea, the payout goes to whoever submitted it first — through this form or a comment on one of our videos.
